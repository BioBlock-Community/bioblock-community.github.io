What is our community?

United by the belief that transparency, collaboration, and data integrity are essential to scientific progress, BioBlock works at the intersection of life sciences, data science, and decentralized technology.

Whether you are developing dApps, advancing FAIR and dFAIR data standards, or designing smart contracts for research compliance, BioBlock provides tools, infrastructure, and support to make your work impactful and interoperable.

  • Access to grant calls and community-led funding
  • Participation in open governance and on-chain decision-making
  • Expert guidance in data protection, blockchain, and AI readiness
  • Collaboration with leading institutions and innovative startups
  • A trusted ecosystem for sharing and certifying research
