Data protection

Privacy Policy

How personal data is handled when you interact with the BioBlock website and team.

Who we are

This website represents the publicly funded BioBlock project, coordinated by Technische Universität Berlin as part of the DATIpilot initiative and funded by the Bundesministerium für Forschung, Technologie und Raumfahrt (BMFTR). Website: www.bio-block.org.

What personal data we collect and why

Contact forms

If you use a contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent securely to the BioBlock team so that we can respond. We do not store personal data on this website’s server.

Embedded or linked external content

Pages may link to or embed content from external platforms, including Google Calendar, LinkedIn, Slack, scientific publications, and other websites. These platforms may collect data, use cookies, embed tracking technologies, or store information about your interaction. BioBlock does not control or access data collected through these services; refer to each service’s privacy policy for details.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies for analytics or advertising. External services or embedded media may set cookies, which you can disable in your browser settings.

Who we share data with

We do not share your data with third parties. Data submitted by email or contact form is handled by the project team and stored securely for correspondence only.

How long we retain data

Contact enquiries are retained only as long as needed to respond and for project documentation required under public-funding guidelines.

Your rights under GDPR

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, correction or deletion, restriction of processing, or object to its use. To make a request, email mariano.n.cruzbournazou@tu-berlin.de.

Where data is processed

This website is hosted in Germany. Communication is handled by team members at TU Berlin and project partners within the European Union.

Legal basis for processing

We process data based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and our legitimate interest in public project communication under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

External links and responsibility

This site may link to external websites. We are not responsible for their content, functionality, or privacy policies.
