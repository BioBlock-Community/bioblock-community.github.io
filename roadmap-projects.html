Management project

MT — Management of a Decentralized Innovation Community (StEDI)Steuerung einer Dezentralen Innovationscommunity

StEDI creates an innovative, decentralized transfer space for collaboration, knowledge exchange, and sustainable innovation in biotechnology. It is building a self-sustaining, transparent, and trustworthy platform for research and development based on Open Science principles.

Core activities

  • Goal system and monitoring: define and adapt mission statements, KPIs, and monitoring for innovation processes.
  • Strategy and program development: establish governance rules, business models, long-term sustainability, and an implementation roadmap.
  • Communication and innovation management: strengthen internal and external communication, networking, and transfer of research into industry.
  • IT infrastructure: build a scalable platform for blockchain technology and server-based applications, from requirements through verification and use cases.

Through close collaboration across science, industry, and society, StEDI promotes blockchain- and data-driven innovation and builds broad acceptance for the BioBlock approach.

Community project 01

BloKSI — Blockchain Konsortium als Soziale Innovation

BloKSI promotes new business models through secure exchange of FAIR data and knowledge services. Its blockchain-based infrastructure is designed to provide trust, traceability, and data privacy in bioprocess development.

The consortium jointly defines architecture, transaction logic, and digital processes. Its work includes cross-organizational FAIR data provision, experiment reproducibility, membership governance, a badging system for provenance, cloud infrastructure for data-intensive workflows, and a provenance collector for collaborative bioprocess development.

The roadmap addresses smart contracts, cryptocurrencies, platform scalability, and decision-making mechanisms that include customers, providers, and prosumers.

Community project 02

dCoP — Dezentrale Communities of Practice

dCoP creates a connected and effective biotechnology community with robust structures and optimized processes. It develops standards and strategies for blockchain use in science and establishes common processes for the BioBlock platform.

The project focuses on meaningful, sustainable integration of blockchain technologies into scientific workflows, shaped around industry requirements and use cases. Its knowledge exchange platform supports FAIR data management and transparent, secure, and sustainable transfer—creating an environment in which access to knowledge and collaboration accelerate innovation.
