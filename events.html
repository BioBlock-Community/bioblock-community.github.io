2025–2026

Project timeline

  1. 01 Jan 2025

    Official start of StEDI

    The BioBlock Management Team project runs throughout the funding period and ensures governance compliance, roadmap execution, and community development.

  2. 12 Sep–10 Oct 2025

    First BioBlock Call for Proposals

    The first open call invited community projects advancing FAIR data and decentralized collaboration. Read the archived call details.

  3. 01 Oct 2025

    Start of CP1 and CP2

    BloKSI and dCoP were funded as part of the initial BioBlock proposal accepted in 2024.

  4. 24 Oct 2025

    First BioBlock Great Council Meeting

    The community met at CIC Venture Café Berlin for proposal pitches, awards, and networking.

  5. Q1/Q2 2026

    Second BioBlock Call for Proposals

    The second call was scheduled for the first half of 2026.

  6. Q2 2026

    Start of CP3, CP4, and CP5

    Community projects chosen and accepted at the first Great Council meeting begin their work.
