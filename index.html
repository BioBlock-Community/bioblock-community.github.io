Community

Who we are

BioBlock is a community of scientists, technologists, and innovators working to transform biotechnology through secure, transparent, and decentralized data infrastructure.

We bring together universities, startups, industry leaders, and public institutions to create a blockchain-based ecosystem that empowers trustworthy collaboration, FAIR data practices, and responsible innovation. Rooted in science and driven by open-source principles, BioBlock exists to protect intellectual property.
