Second BioBlock Call for Proposals

BioBlock Open Call for Proposals 2026

BioBlock invites projects that strengthen open, verifiable, and FAIR data exchange across the biotechnology value chain.

Funding lines & budget

  • Community Sprints: up to 18 months; up to €150,000 per partner. A maximum of two partners may participate, with at least one academic partner.
  • Praxis Sprints: up to 18 months; up to €250,000 per academic partner and up to €150,000 per non-academic partner. At least one academic and one non-academic partner are required.

The stated budgets follow the DATIpilot project formats. Individual challenges may include specific restrictions that must be observed and complied with.

Announced funding budget: All stated amounts explicitly include any eligible 10–20% overhead for eligible institutions, such as universities and research institutions.

Challenges

Each proposal must address one of the following challenges:

  1. BioBlock Community Knowledge Base
  2. AI Tool for FAIR Data Generation
  3. Enabling Agentic-AI Automated Negotiation, Dispute Resolution and Copyright Protection for Data Trading via Smart Contract Development
  4. Community-Driven Metadata and Data Standards for Bioprocess Engineering
  5. Legal, Regulatory and Cybersecurity Framework for Trusted Data Exchange

Eligibility

Academic organizations, including universities and research institutions, and non-academic organizations, including companies, non-profit organizations, local authorities, foundations, associations, federations, and educational institutions, may apply. Only entities with a legal seat in Germany are eligible to receive DATIpilot funding.

Other entities may participate as associated partners without direct funding or reporting obligations, or as subcontractors under procurement law. Project results must be exploited in Germany, the European Economic Area, or Switzerland.

Timeline

  • Call opening: 21 September 2026
  • Submission deadline: 29 October 2026
  • Pre-selection notification: by 27 November 2026
  • Interviews: Friday, 4 December 2026, 09:00–15:00 CET
  • Final selection notification: by 14 December 2026
  • easy-Online submission: by 31 January 2027 for selected projects
  • Expected project start: 1 May 2027
  • Expected project completion: by 31 October 2028
Interview format: shortlisted applicants will present their proposal online before the BioBlock Great Council, followed by a Q&A session. The final presentation format will be communicated in due course.

Application process

  1. Download and complete the official proposal template. Submit the proposal in English and include short CVs of the principal investigator(s), with a maximum of two pages per CV.
  2. Combine the completed template and CVs in one PDF. An optional technical or overview illustration may be included.
  3. Name the submitted file 2026_BioBlockCall_ProjectAcronym_LeadInstitution.pdf and send it to submission@bioblock-community.org.
  4. Selected projects must submit a full proposal in German through easy-Online to BMFTR/PtJ by 31 January 2027.

Governance & evaluation

  • Management Team: preliminary fit-to-call screening for formal compliance.
  • BioBlock Great Council: evaluation against excellence, impact, and quality and efficiency. Each criterion is scored from 0 to 5.
  • Interviews: shortlisted projects present online before the BioBlock Great Council on 4 December 2026, followed by Q&A.
  • PtJ: legal and financial eligibility check for selected projects.
  • Final decision: grant agreements follow PtJ approval.

Terms

Funding is provided as a non-repayable grant under the DATIpilot programme framework. Funded projects must meet requirements on reporting, Open Access, science communication, FAIR data, intellectual property, GDPR, state aid, export control, and audit. Funded projects must acknowledge BMFTR, DATIpilot, and BioBlock.
