What is DATIpilot?

DATIpilot, initiated by the German Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR), is a pilot funding scheme designed to accelerate the transfer of research into practical applications and foster innovation. It is an experimental platform that helps prepare the future German Agency for Transfer and Innovation (DATI) and modernize funding processes.

The program has two modules: Innovation Sprints, which rapidly implement concrete transfer ideas within up to 18 months, and Innovation Communities, which support long-term collaborative innovation projects for up to four years. Both emphasize openness, co-creation, and agile processes across academia, industry, and society.
